BRAND IDENTITY & EMAIL NEWSLETTER DESIGN

The S.A.V.E. Women's Foundation

Newsletter Brand Identity & Redesign

In 2022, I redesigned the S.A.V.E Women's Foundation newsletter to create a cleaner, more cohesive expereince that improved readability, strengthened the organization's identity, and made important information easier for readers to engage with.

The Challenge

The original newsletter used bright pink accents, inconsistent section styles, and varied typography, which created visual clutter and made the content harder to scan.

The goal was to create a calmer, more cohesive visual system that would strengthen brand recognition and improve readability.

  • Improve Readability
  • Reduce Visual Clutter
  • Establish a consistent color palette
  • Create clear typography rules
  • Strengthen visual hierarchy
  • Make calls to action easier to identify

Before & After

The orignal newsletter used dense text, inconsistent spacing, and competing visual elements, which made the content difficult to scan. The donation message was present, but it blended into the surrounding information.
The redesign introduced clearer hierarchy, improved spacing, and more focused content sections for easier reading. The donation area was separated into a dedicated call-to-action with clear giving options and a prominent support button.

Project Reflection

This project taught me that a strong design system can elevate an organization's message without changing who they are. By refining the color palette, establishing typography rules, and creating reusable sections, I was able to deliver a newsletter that is clear, more intentional, and easier for readers to engage with.
