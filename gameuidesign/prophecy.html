PROJECT OVERVIEW

Prophesy was a personal exploration into interface design for an independent fantasy RPG. I wanted to explore how visual identity, navigation, iconography, character systems, and motion could work together to create a cohesive game experience.

REFLECTION

Prophecy was an early exploration that spaeked my interest in desining interfaces as systems rather than isolated screens. It gave me the opportunity to experiment with visual hierarchy, iconography, game menus, motion design, and world-building while learning how much interface design can shape the personality of a game.
